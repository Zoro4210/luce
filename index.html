The exhibition

Four centuries ago, a portrait was already a kind of interface.

Paint, posture, clothing, and light were arranged to transmit identity. L’Archivio Luminoso follows these visual codes from the Renaissance into our own image-saturated moment.

Looking is never neutral.

Renaissance artists understood that every portrait stages a negotiation between sitter, maker, and viewer. This exhibition draws that triangle into the present—where every screen has become a frame and every image asks to be read.

Across four intimate chapters, details become clues: a fur cuff, a half-smile, a lens held to the light. Slow down. The works will meet you halfway.
